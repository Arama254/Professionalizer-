AI-powered workflow designed to transform messy mobile shorthand into polished, client-ready professional emails. AI Content Assistant: The Professionalizer
Context
As a professional managing multiple tasks on the go, I often lacked the time to draft polished, formal emails from my mobile device
. I needed a way to instantly transform rapid, messy shorthand notes into professional, client-ready communication to maintain high standards and save time during a busy workday
.
Methodology
I implemented a structured prompting strategy to ensure the AI adopted a specific persona and maintained a consistent professional voice
.
The Framework: I applied the Role + Context + Task + Format framework
:
Role: Professional executive assistant.
Context: Managing rapid, messy mobile notes for formal communication.
Task: Transform shorthand into clear and polite emails.
Format: Output limited to the Subject Line and Email Body.
Refinement: I utilized few-shot prompting, providing three "before and after" examples to teach the AI specific nuances, such as handling rescheduling or informal offers of lunch
.
Output
Before (Messy Note): "Hey Joe, can't come to the 10am. Dog sick. Re-schedule?"
After (Claude’s Email): "Subject: Rescheduling our 10:00 AM meeting. Hi Joe, unfortunately, I have a family emergency involving my pet and cannot make our 10:00 AM meeting. Would you be open to rescheduling for later this week?"
.
Result
Efficiency: Reduced email drafting time by approximately 80%, allowing for professional communication while on the go
.
Quality: Achieved 100% consistency in professional tone across all mobile communications.
Human Judgment: I demonstrated the difference between AI-assisted and AI-dependent work by reviewing and refining outputs to ensure they remained "friendly" rather than "robotic"
.
